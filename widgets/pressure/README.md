# Pressure

Current surface pressure with the change over the next 6 hours, a line chart of the past 12 hours
(solid) and the next 24 hours (dashed, accent colour), and "頭痛注意 · 15時頃に気圧低下" when the
pressure falls by 4 hPa or more within 6 hours, now or later. The header shows the min–max range.
Hidden until the data arrives.

- **needs:** `weather` (uses the `pressure` sub-key: hourly `surface_pressure` from Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0)
- **settings:** `drop_hpa` (4)
